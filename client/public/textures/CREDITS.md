# Texture credits

`oga-atlas.jpg` is the "OpenGameArt" texture set (the Textures toggle in the
pause menu). Every texture in it is **CC0** (public domain) from OpenGameArt.org:
free to use, no attribution required. Credited anyway, with thanks.

Each was center-cropped to a square and scaled to 256 x 256. Tile numbers
are positions in the atlas (16 per row), as listed in
`client/src/graphics/textureAtlas.ts`.
