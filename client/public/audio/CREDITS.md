## Sound effects

`sfx/*.wav` come from Kenney's CC0 packs (https://kenney.nl): RPG Audio,
Impact Sounds and Interface Sounds. They were converted to mono 22 kHz WAV
so they play in every browser. Spell sounds are synthesized in code
(`client/src/audio/synth.ts`).
